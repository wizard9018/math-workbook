Privacy Policy

Math Workbook is a free practice site for school math. This page explains what it stores.

What we collect

  • Your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture, only to sign you in.
  • Your practice progress: which exercises you did and whether each question was answered correctly.

How it is used

Only to let you sign in and to save and restore your progress on any device. We do not sell, share or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

In a database hosted by Supabase. Each account can read only its own progress.

Deleting your data

Email wizard9018@gmail.com and your account and progress will be deleted.
